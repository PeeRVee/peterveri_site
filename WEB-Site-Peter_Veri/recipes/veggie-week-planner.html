Veggie Week Planner

Seven quick, low-carb dinners: mostly vegetarian, with 1–2 wild-caught fish nights. Each makes 2 servings: dinner tonight, lunch tomorrow.

Ingredients are chosen to be found at Walmart, Save-On Foods, Thrifty Foods, Fairway Market, H Mart, Costco (Langford) and Saanich farm stands.

Please read: These recipes may contain ingredients that can cause severe allergic reactions. Please read every ingredient and label carefully to make sure each one is safe for YOU before making any of these recipes. These recipes are provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.
